The Expenses module lets you track one-time and recurring costs associated with individual employees. Expenses feed into pay period calculations and profitability analytics, giving you a complete picture of your operating costs alongside commission payouts and loan revenue.

## Key Capabilities

- **One-time expense tracking** -- Record individual expenses tied to a specific employee with an amount, date, and optional note.
- **Recurring expenses** -- Define expenses that repeat on a Weekly or Monthly schedule, with configurable intervals and optional expiration dates.
- **Pay period integration** -- Expenses can be associated with pay periods so they appear alongside commission calculations. They can also be manually unassigned.
- **Filtering and pagination** -- Filter the expense list by employee and date range, with paginated results for large datasets.
- **Profitability impact** -- Expenses flow into the Analytics > Profitability module, where they are subtracted from revenue alongside commissions to calculate net profit and margins.

## Data Model

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `employee` | The employee the expense is assigned to (required) |
| `amount` | Dollar amount of the expense (required) |
| `note` | Optional free-text description |
| `date` | Optional date the expense was incurred |
| `payPeriod` | Optional association with a pay period |
| `isManuallyUnassigned` | Flag indicating the expense was explicitly removed from a pay period |
| `recurringExpense` | Link back to the recurring expense that generated this record, if applicable |
