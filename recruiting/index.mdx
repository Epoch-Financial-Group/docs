The Recruiting module provides a complete hiring pipeline for mortgage companies to identify, evaluate, and onboard new team members. From first contact through their first day on the job, Keystone tracks every candidate, their licensing, production history, compensation modeling, and the conversion process that turns a candidate into a fully set up employee.
