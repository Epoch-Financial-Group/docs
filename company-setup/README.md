# Company Setup

Before you can begin tracking loans, running commissions, or generating reports in EpochOS, you need to configure the foundational data that the rest of the platform depends on. This section walks through every piece of that initial setup.

## What you will configure

| Area | Description | Link |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Company Settings** | Company name, NMLS number, Loan Origination System, and payroll configuration | [Company Settings](company-settings.md) |
| **Branches** | Physical or organizational locations where your company operates | [Branches](branches.md) |
| **Employees** | The people who originate, process, and manage loans | [Employees](employees.md) |
| **Lenders** | Wholesale lenders and correspondent partners you submit loans to | [Lenders](lenders.md) |
| **Lead Sources** | Where your loans originate from -- used in tracking and commission rules | [Lead Sources](lead-sources.md) |

## Recommended setup order

While you can configure these in any order, the following sequence avoids having to go back and fill in references later:

1. **Company Settings** -- Set your company name, NMLS, LOS, and payroll schedules first.
2. **Branches** -- Create your branch locations before adding employees (employees are assigned to branches).
3. **Employees** -- Add your team members. You will need at least one branch if you want to assign them during creation.
4. **Lenders** -- Add the wholesale lenders and correspondents you work with.
5. **Lead Sources** -- Define where your loans come from (Zillow, Realtor referrals, etc.).

After completing these steps you will be ready to import or manually enter loans, configure commission templates, and run your first pay period.

## Where to find these settings

All Company Setup features are accessed through the left-hand sidebar:

- **Administration > Company Settings** -- Global company configuration and payroll settings
- **Administration > Branches** -- Branch management
- **Directories > People** -- Employee directory
- **Directories > Lenders** -- Lender directory
- Lead sources are managed within **Administration > Company Settings**
