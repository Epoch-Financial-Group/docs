title: "First Login"

---

## Creating your account

Go to [app.keystn.com](https://app.keystn.com) and click **Create an account**. You will need a corporate email address — personal email providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, etc.) are not accepted.

Fill in your name, email, and a password, then click **Create account**.

## Setting up your company

After signing in for the first time, you will be prompted to create your company workspace. Enter your company name and complete the setup wizard. This creates your workspace at a unique URL your team will use to log in.

## Returning logins

On subsequent visits, go to [app.keystn.com](https://app.keystn.com) and sign in with your email and password.

Once you are in, follow the [Initial Setup](/getting-started/initial-setup) guide to configure your company before inviting your team.
