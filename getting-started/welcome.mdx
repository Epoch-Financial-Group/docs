Keystone is your company's operations platform for managing the mortgage pipeline. This guide covers what you'll use day-to-day as a team member.

As a loan officer, processor, or branch manager, you can view and work the loan pipeline, read company policies, and manage your assigned tasks. Your company administrator handles configuration, commissions, and accounting.

## What's in this guide

- **[First Login](/getting-started/first-login)** — Signing in after your administrator invites you
- **[Navigation](/getting-started/navigation)** — Finding your way around
- **[Loan Pipeline](/loan-pipeline/overview)** — Viewing and working your loans
- **[Policies](/policies/overview)** — Reading company policies
- **[Tasks](/tasks/overview)** — Completing assigned tasks

Need access to something you can't see? Contact your company administrator.
